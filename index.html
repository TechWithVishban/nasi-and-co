About Nasi & Co.

Nasi & Co. is a contemporary Malaysian food concept celebrating traditional Malaysian comfort food. We take the dishes many of us grew up with and prepare them thoughtfully, with quality ingredients, balanced flavours and a modern sense of hospitality.

Every plate is designed to feel familiar, generous and made with care.
